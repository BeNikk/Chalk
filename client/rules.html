Community Rules

Chalk is a campus writing wall. To keep it thoughtful, safe, and honest, we ask that you follow a few simple guidelines.

01

Be respectful.

Disagreements are fine, but harassment, hate speech, and personal attacks are not. Remember there is a real person behind every post.

02

Keep it human.

This is a space for original thoughts, rants, and poems. Spam, bot activity, and purely promotional content will be removed.

03

Protect privacy.

Do not share anyone's personal information, screenshots of private messages, or identifying details without their explicit permission.

04

Label accurately.

Use the labels to say what the thing is. Don't use them to bypass the rules or mislead readers about the content of your post.

Failure to follow these guidelines may result in your posts being removed or your account being suspended.
