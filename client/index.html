How names work

  • You log in with your account, so everyone here is from campus.
  • You post under a handle. Other students only ever see the handle.
  • Moderators can see which account is behind a handle, but only look in serious cases. It's in the rules.
  • It's a nickname, not a mask. On a small campus, people may still recognise how you write.
