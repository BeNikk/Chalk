The rules

Chalk is a small corner of the internet for our campus. Keep it a place people want to write in.

Be kind

  • Rant about things, not people. No negative posts about named individuals, students or staff.
  • Don't share photos of other people without their permission. That includes profile photos: use one of yourself.
  • No bullying, threats, hate, or anything that targets someone for who they are.
  • Disagree in the comments like you would face to face.

Use the labels

Every post gets one: #rant #poetry #mess #music #fixit. Pick the one that fits, so people can find the mood they're in. For #fixit, say what's broken and where, so it can actually get fixed.

How names work

  • You sign up with your email address. It's never shown on the site.
  • You post under a handle. Everyone else only sees the handle.
  • The people who run Chalk can see which email is behind a handle. They only look in serious cases, like threats or someone being harmed.
  • It's a nickname, not a mask. On a small campus, people may still recognise how you write.

Moderators

Moderators are people chosen by the admin. They have a Moderator badge on their profile, and they can delete any post or comment that breaks these rules. If someone keeps breaking them, the people running Chalk can remove their account.

See something that breaks the rules? Tell a moderator or one of the open source guild's organisers in person. (A report button is on the list of things to build.)

Your stuff

You can delete your own posts and comments at any time, and they're gone for everyone.
