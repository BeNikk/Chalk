## Adding your own drawing

We'd love drawings by students. Any Open Peeps figure on the site can be swapped for a student's drawing.

1. **Draw it** in the Chalk style:
   - Black lines on a transparent background.
   - Optionally one flat fill from our soft colors: `#fbe4e1` (red), `#ece7f8` (purple), `#e2efe3` (green), `#ddebf6` (blue), `#fbf1cc` (yellow).
   - People doing everyday campus things: writing, reading, listening to music, carrying boxes, waiting in a queue…
2. **Export it as SVG.** Free tools that can do this: [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/), [Figma](https://figma.com/), or draw on paper and trace it with Inkscape's *Trace Bitmap*.
3. **Keep it small.** Under 100 KB please. Simplify paths if your tool has that option.
4. **Name it** with lowercase words and dashes, describing what it shows: `carrying-boxes.svg`, not `drawing1.svg`.
5. **It must be yours** (or CC0). Don't trace someone else's art, and don't copy Notion's illustrations.
6. **Add a row to the table above** and open a pull request with a screenshot of where you used it.

When you use a drawing on a page, give it `alt=""` if it's only decoration, or a short description if it adds meaning.
